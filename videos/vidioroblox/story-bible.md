# Story bible — "Sahabat & Harta"

- Core premise: a best friend who promised to always be there steals a shared treasure — then reveals why.
- Main character goal: share the adventure / treasure with his best friend.
- Conflict/stakes: betrayal; trapped behind a sealed cave door.
- Emotional arc: warmth -> wonder -> shock -> desperation -> anger/vow -> loneliness -> devastation (guilt/understanding).
- Turning point/reveal: the friend stole to pay for his mother's medicine.
- Ending/payoff: main character reading the one-sentence message, devastated.
- Assumption: the final room is the main character's bedroom (script gives no location for "3 days later").
